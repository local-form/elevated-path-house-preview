Work and purpose

A job is part of recovery here.

Residents who are able to work are expected to find a job within 30 days. Jon helps with résumé building and keeps the job search accountable.

Fitness and strength

Rebuild the body, too.

Jon is a certified personal trainer. Residents train at least three days a week with programming that builds healthy routines, subject to any medical limits.

House commitments

What every resident agrees to.

Each resident initials these before moving in.

01

Stay substance-free

No alcohol, non-prescribed drugs or prohibited substances, in or out of the house.

02

Random testing

Random and reasonable-suspicion drug and alcohol tests. Refusing or tampering can mean discharge.

03

Show up

Four or more recovery meetings a week, the weekly house and group meetings, and check-ins with Jon.

04

Follow house policies

Curfew, guests, overnight passes, chores and conduct.

05

Follow your aftercare plan

IOP, outpatient or other recommended care continues while you live here.

06

Respect the house

Relapse, violence, threats, prohibited items or an unapproved night away may lead to discharge.

What this isn't

A sober home, not a treatment center.

Elevated Path House is a nonclinical recovery residence for men who are medically stable and don't need detox. There's no detox, medical care, therapy or clinical treatment on site.

  • Aftercare and IOP friendly
  • Weekly house and group meetings
  • Random drug and alcohol testing
  • Employment accountability
  • Fitness programming
  • Respectful peer environment
