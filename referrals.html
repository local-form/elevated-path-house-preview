Who's a fit

Men who are stable and ready.

  • Medically stable, no detox needed
  • Ready for a nonclinical, structured home
  • Has an aftercare or IOP plan, or will follow one
  • Working, or will find work within 30 days

The interview

What Jon reviews.

Sobriety, stability, aftercare, work plans and readiness for a structured home. Approved applicants review the house rules, testing and resident agreement before move-in.

With the resident's separate written consent, Jon can contact the listed treatment or aftercare provider to verify participation or coordinate admission.

What your client gets

Structure that supports your plan.

01

Structured sober living

Clear expectations, curfew, random drug and alcohol testing, house meetings and shared accountability.

02

Recovery coaching

Weekly group support and individual check-ins with Recovery Jon focused on consistency and forward movement.

03

Fitness and strength

Personal training guidance and workout programming that helps residents rebuild healthy routines.

04

Employment support

Résumé building, job-search accountability and a 30-day expectation for residents who are able to work.

05

Recovery community

At least four AA, NA or approved recovery meetings each week, scheduled around work and aftercare.

06

Purposeful connection

Two outings or shared dinners each month to create community beyond the walls of the house.
