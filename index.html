More questions

Is Elevated Path House a treatment center?

No. It is a nonclinical sober living residence. It does not provide detoxification, medical care, therapy or licensed substance-use treatment.

Can residents attend IOP or outpatient treatment?

Yes. Residents are encouraged to follow an appropriate aftercare plan, and work and meeting expectations can be arranged around an established treatment schedule.

What recovery participation is expected?

Residents attend at least four AA, NA or other approved recovery-support meetings weekly, plus mandatory house and group meetings and individual check-ins with Recovery Jon.
